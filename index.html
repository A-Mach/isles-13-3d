Concept walkthrough

The Isles 13

A short proof of what a walkable 3D model could add to a listing. Open it, walk through the rooms and look around.

Open the walkthrough

Best on a phone held sideways, or on a laptop. The first load takes a few seconds and needs an internet connection.

What you can do

Switch between an aerial view and walking through the house. Tap a room name to be walked there, or tap a glowing doorway to step through it. Drag to look around.

What it is not

It is not the real house. The layout was drawn from the listing's photographs and description, and the furniture, finishes and artworks are placeholders. It demonstrates the experience; it is not a record of the property.

What a real one would need

A measured floor plan or a scan of the home, photographs of every room, the actual finishes, and a decision on which furniture stays. With those, the same walkthrough could match the house room for room.
