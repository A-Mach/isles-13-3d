    About the property

    Set within The Isles at Dorado Beach Resort, this one-story residence presents an elegant expression of space, privacy, and indoor-outdoor living. Built in 2022, the home sits on a 1,451 SQMT lot, surrounded by water and landscape views that define the setting from the moment you arrive.

    A double-height open layout anchors the main living area, where natural light, clean lines, and refined finishes create a sense of scale without losing warmth.

    The living room, dining area, and kitchen connect in one continuous flow, opening fully to the terrace and outdoor lounge. Large sliding glass panels remove the barrier between interior and exterior, allowing the home to function as a single, extended living space.

    The kitchen features a large culinary workstation and entertainment island, bespoke cabinetry, and integrated luxury appliances. It connects directly to the expansive outdoor BBQ terrace, creating an organic and natural transition for entertaining.

    The residence includes four bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom, plus a flexible additional room suitable for an office, media room, or guest space.

    The primary suite is positioned to capture open views and direct access to the outdoor areas, complemented by a spacious bathroom and a layout focused on comfort and privacy.

    Outside, a covered outdoor living and dining area leads to an inviting pool deck with an infinity-edge pool and jacuzzi, all oriented toward the lake and the Dorado Beach TPC Sugarcane golf course. The landscaping frames the property while maintaining open sightlines, reinforcing both privacy and connection to the surroundings.

    Additional features include a powder room, pool bath, full air-conditioning system, water softener, cistern, and a community-wide generator. The residence is offered furnished, with interiors curated by Palacios to align with the home's architectural language.

    Located within The Isles, one of the most acclaimed communities inside Dorado Beach Resort, residents benefit from a gated environment with access to internal amenities including pools, tennis courts, gym, and recreational spaces. Resort membership further expands the lifestyle to include TPC championship golf courses, the Encanto Beach Club, the Watermill pool and amenities, a spa, fitness facilities, over five dining venues, the Rockefeller nature trail and an extraordinary community.

    This residence combines scale, design, and location within a setting defined by water, landscape, and access to one of Puerto Rico's most established resort communities in the Caribbean.

    Dorado

    Dorado Beach Resort and the communities around it

    Dorado is where the largest share of LDRE's work sits, across both active and closed listings — including LDRE's two highest-value current properties, Plantation Village PH and The Isles 13. It's also one of Cristina's three stated markets, with a professional history that includes work with the Dorado Beach Ritz-Carlton Reserve.

    Communities within Dorado
    Dorado Beach Resort — Plantation Village, The Isles and Dorado Beach East — along with Sabanera Dorado, Dorado Reef, The Greens, Dorado del Mar, Cielo Dorado Village and Villa Dorado Estates.
    What it costs
    Current LDRE listings in Dorado range from $4,300,000 to $11,700,000.

    Living in a Dorado resort community

    Homes here are typically gated, with full community generators, private cisterns and 24-hour security — practical realities of island living that LDRE's own listings document consistently. Resort membership at Dorado Beach Resort extends further, into TPC championship golf courses, the Encanto Beach Club, the Watermill pool and amenities, a spa, five-plus dining venues and the Rockefeller nature trail.
